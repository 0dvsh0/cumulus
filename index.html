Honest scoring

An untested check is never a failure.

Seven distinct outcomes. Only three move your score. The rest are reported plainly — alongside coverage, so you always know how much of the sky was actually visible.

PassVerified working. Full credit.
PartialWorks with caveats. Half credit.
FailVerified broken. No credit.
BlockedCouldn't test: robots.txt, a challenge, or a prerequisite failed.
InconclusiveTested, but the evidence didn't settle it.
ErrorThe audit itself hit a problem (network, timeout).
N/ADoesn't apply to this site. Excluded.

Safety & trust

A polite guest in someone else's sky.

Every submitted URL is treated as untrusted. Cumulus reads; it never writes.

  • No internal targetsIP literals, localhost, private and reserved ranges and internal hostnames are refused; every redirect hop is re-checked.
  • Robots.txt respectedIf your robots.txt disallows the auditor, those checks are marked Blocked — never Failed.
  • Hard limitsA capped number of fetches and renders, size limits per response, timeouts and a total time budget.
  • No forms, no accounts, no paymentsCheckout counts as "reached" when a payment interface or account step loads. Then the agent stops.
